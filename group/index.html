The Group

CBI Group is an engineering group that designs and manufactures GFRP reinforcement. It is organised around three high-level pillars.

  1. 01

    Holding

    EU holding — Estonia

    CBIE (Composite Building Innovation Estonia OÜ) is the EU holding company of the group.

  2. 02

    Manufacturing

    Manufacturing — Tunisia

    The operating company in Tunisia produces GFRP rebar and mesh, sold under the PROBAR (Professional Rebar) and PROMESH brands.

  3. 03

    Commercial

    European commercial presence

    Serving EU customers and distributors with products exported duty-free from Tunisia.
