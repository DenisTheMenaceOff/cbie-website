Who we are

An EU holding with manufacturing in Tunisia

CBI Group is an engineering group that built the GFRP reinforcement market in Tunisia from the ground up and is now expanding internationally. CBIE — Composite Building Innovation Estonia OÜ — is its EU holding company.

GFRP — Glass Fibre Reinforced Polymer — rebar and mesh is a non-corroding, lightweight alternative to steel reinforcement in concrete, used in construction and shipbuilding.

Why GFRP

A durable alternative to steel reinforcement

Corrosion-resistant

Does not rust or degrade in saline, humid or chemically aggressive environments; ideal for coastal, marine and industrial use.

Non-conductive

Electrically and magnetically neutral; suited to tunnels, substations and areas near sensitive equipment.

Longer service life

No corrosion means lower maintenance and lifecycle cost over decades.

Lightweight

Up to 5× lighter than steel, cutting transport, labour and installation time.

High mechanical strength

Excellent tensile strength and rigidity.

Eco-friendly

Lower embodied carbon and energy in production; recyclable at end of life as concrete aggregate.
