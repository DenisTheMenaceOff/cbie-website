Certifications & Quality

Certification is the group's key competitive asset: it opens regulated construction markets that uncertified GFRP cannot enter.

Certified

ETA — European Technical Assessment

Issued by
ITAB / ITC-CNR — Italian Technical Assessment Body, member of EOTA
Reference
ETA 24/0295 of 27 June 2024; EAD 260023-00-0301; AVCP system 1+
Scope
PROBAR glass FRP bars as reinforcement of structural elements; straight bars Ø6–16 mm.
What it confirms
Assessment of PROBAR for the EU construction market under Regulation (EU) No 305/2011.
Markets it opens
European Union.
Certified

EPD — Environmental Product Declaration

Issued by
The International EPD® System (programme operator EPD International AB); licensee EPD Tunisia
Reference
EPD-IES-0029299; version 17 March 2026, valid until 16 March 2031
Scope
PROBAR fiberglass rebar, in accordance with ISO 14025 and EN 15804+A2.
What it confirms
Verified environmental performance of the product.
Markets it opens
EU green procurement (EU Green Deal; Construction Products Regulation 2026–2027).
Certified

ISO 9001:2015

Issued by
RoyalCert International Registrars
Reference
Certificate No. 08655/CBIO9U; initial certification 23 September 2024
Scope
Manufacture and sale of fiberglass bars and mesh.
What it confirms
Quality management system.
Markets it opens
All markets; baseline requirement for institutional buyers.
Certified

INNORPI (Tunisia)

Issued by
INNORPI — Institut National de la Normalisation et de la Propriété Industrielle

National conformity.
